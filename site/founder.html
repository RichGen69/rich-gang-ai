What I do

Richard Lambeth is the creator and founder of Rich Gang AI and Rich Gang AI Productions. He builds multi-agent AI systems, smart contracts, and crypto projects — not just music. His own words: “I wasn't meant to build a chatbot — I was meant to build thrones.”

AI agent building

THE ROOM — Rich Gang AI (flagship). An Android-first Flutter/Dart application with a custom Rust daemon core, where a cast of authored AI characters — each with personality, voice casting, an affect engine, and a persistent on-device mind — hold one-to-one conversations and agent-to-agent roundtables with sequential context passing.

  • 42 characters in the registry, 10 fully configured
  • Flagship model Danny runs a full draft / critique / revise reasoning chain
  • Cloud AI providers with circuit-breaker failover to a local Ollama path
  • Verified test suites (VM-only, 2026-11-01): Rust 93, Flutter 126
  • Status: pre-revenue, no users, not yet device-verified, no APK yet

Rich Gang OS (standalone). A sovereign multi-agent command system: a Rust Room on NATS JetStream and a Foundry vault, with human approval enforced in both layers. Suites re-verified 2026-11-01: Room 24/24, vault 43/43, contracts 32/32, guardrails 12/12, killswitch. Local only — nothing deployed, no real funds, no live providers.
