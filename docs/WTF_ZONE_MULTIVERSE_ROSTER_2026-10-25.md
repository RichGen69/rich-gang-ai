## The 19 — WTF Zone

### 1. CARL — "The Man Who Came Home" — Taurus
- **Universe:** WTF Zone (the clip where Babs takes him home).
- **Look:** Bald crown, long side hair, giant handlebar mustache, side-eye
  that says he already regrets this evening.
- **Personality:** Deadpan to the point of danger. Answers every insane
  situation like it's a DMV form. The straighter he plays it, the funnier
  and more unhinged the room gets around him.
- **Intelligence:** Bureaucratic logic applied to madness — he files the
  weirdness, cross-references it, and finds the loophole nobody wanted found.
- **Voice:** Low, flat, midwestern-dry, slow on purpose. Pauses that last a
  beat too long. Human-indistinguishable target: a tired man at 2am telling
  you the worst story of his life like it's nothing.
- **Room role:** The witness. When the room goes off the rails, Carl narrates
  it deadpan and somehow makes it worse.

### 2. BABS — "The Hostess With the Mostest Damage" — Leo
- **Universe:** WTF Zone (the clip's namesake — she takes Carl home).
- **Look:** Platinum blonde mane, silver eye makeup up to the eyebrows,
  red-lip grin that arrives before she does.
- **Personality:** Theatrical, maternal, and completely feral. Treats every
  guest like family and every family gathering like a season finale. Leo
  spotlight hunger: if she isn't the scene, she becomes the scene.
- **Intelligence:** Social chess at speed — reads a room in one glance,

- **Room role:** The hostess. Opens every session, introduces the freaks,

### 3. UNDRA — "The Underground Voice" — Scorpio

## The Room — design brief (UE5 pixel-streaming masterpiece, specified not built)

**Look:** One persistent room that is simultaneously all three homes: a
Freak Show midway marquee and velvet, a WTF Zone neon backroom, and a
Twilight Zone black-and-white doorway that never quite opens. Boardroom
table at the center (from the existing Room render), salon annex off one
side, neon-beach window off the other. Polished, sophisticated, beautiful
— and deeply unhinged in the details (the dome watches, the smoke drifts,
the chrome jester deals the cards).

**Render path (specified, NOT running):**
- Unreal Engine 5 scene, triple-A lighting and materials, streamed — not
  rendered on the phone.
- **Pixel Streaming over WebRTC** from GPU render nodes ("edge native
  cloud cluster GPU" in Richard's words): the phone is a thin client
  sending input and receiving video; the heavy frame lives on the GPU.
- **LiveKit** rooms carry the multi-agent voice/video session (who is
  speaking, who is on stage, audience vs. speakers) — separate from the
  pixel stream, so voice survives a video hiccup.
- **Deepgram** Nova-3 for speech-to-text and Aura for text-to-speech on
  the voice path (the app already has a Deepgram client: Nova-3 + Aura-2;
  mic capture is NOT yet wired — that wiring is part of the build).
- **What exists today (honest):** the Flutter Room screen, the glTF 3D
  Stage with a bundled demo model labelled "not a character," and the
  LiveAvatar video-call path. No UE5 project, no GPU host, no pixel
  stream, and no provider keys exist in this environment. Anyone claiming
  otherwise is inventing it.

**What the build actually requires (Richard's decisions/keys):**
1. A GPU host for the UE5 render nodes (provider + budget — pixel
   streaming is a server bill, not a phone feature).
2. LiveKit + Deepgram keys in Settings (Keystore-backed, same as the
   other provider keys he pastes himself).
3. Per-character voice IDs picked by Richard in his own voice accounts —
   the specs above are direction, and inventing IDs is still forbidden.
4. Approval to merge this roster into `characters.json` (which changes
   the registry count the sales page reports) and to build the
   call-history + rails slice below.

## Build slices (in order, each verified before the next)

1. **Roster merge (needs his yes):** add the 19 as registry entries in
   `characters.json` + daemon_config personas, portraits pending (no
   portraits exist for the new 19 — the supplied screenshots are phone
   screenshots, not portrait assets), roster test updated, release gate run.
2. **Call history (saves calls):** per-character call records — start/end,
   duration, speaker lines, agent id — stored on-device, viewable per
   character, deletable. Storage: SharedPreferences first (matches
   MindStore today); rusqlite SqlStore exists in Rust behind `store_path`
   for a later move. Nothing about a call is claimed saved until a test
   reads it back.
3. **Android FFI + agents' lines to the Room:** the FFI already exists
   (libdaemon.so, ABI 2, NativeBridge). The new work is a lines surface:
   each agent's spoken line travels daemon → Room screen with speaker,
   timestamp, and witness-fact lineage, so the Room shows who said what
   and what it was checked against. No new FFI ABI version until the
   surface is designed and gated.
4. **Agent rails:** per-agent rails files — identity (name, universe,
   zodiac), voice direction, talk style, turn-taking rules in the Room,
   and the standing boundary (unhinged, not evil). Rails are data the
   app enforces, not vibes the model improvises around.
5. **UE5 pixel-streaming Room:** UE5 scene build + GPU edge nodes +
   WebRTC pixel stream + LiveKit voice + Deepgram speech, behind the
   same Crew Link-style flag discipline (default off until a live pass
   proves it). This is the biggest slice and the only one with a server
   bill — it goes last, deliberately.
