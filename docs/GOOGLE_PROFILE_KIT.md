## How Google panels actually work (read before expecting results)

- A Knowledge Panel / Business Profile is NOT edited by asking
  Google: it is generated from authoritative sources — this
  website (with the Organization structured data now on it),
  the Business Profile itself, and consistent social profiles.
- Creating the profile is step one; **verification is a separate
  step Google controls** (postcard to a business address, phone,
  or video verification, depending on what it offers). Only
  Richard can complete verification — the code arrives to him.
- After verification, Google re-indexes on its own schedule
  (days to weeks). Nothing here makes results appear instantly.
- Never publish a street address, phone number, or handle that
  Richard has not supplied. Never claim "best", "first", or
  "ahead of everyone" in the profile — Google removes misleading
  business content, and the verified work above is what makes the
  capability case.
